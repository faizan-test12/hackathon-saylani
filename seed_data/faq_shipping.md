# Roast & Co. Shipping Policy

Here are the details regarding our shipping policies, processing times, and options.

## Domestic Shipping (US)
We proudly ship across the United States. 
- **Free standard shipping** is automatically applied to all orders over $35. 
- For orders under $35, a $5.99 flat rate for standard shipping will apply.

## Processing Time
We roast our beans in small batches to ensure maximum freshness. Order processing time is typically 1-2 business days before the order is handed over to the carrier.

## Delivery Times
Once your order has been processed, delivery times depend on your chosen shipping method:
- **Standard Shipping:** 3-5 business days.
- **Express Shipping:** 1-2 business days, available for a flat rate of $12.99.

## International Shipping
Currently, we offer international shipping to Canada and the UK.
- **Flat rate:** $15.99 for all international orders.
- **Delivery timeframe:** 7-14 business days, depending on customs processing in the destination country. Please note that customers are responsible for any applicable customs duties or taxes.

## Order Tracking
As soon as your order has been shipped, you will receive an email containing a tracking number so you can follow your coffee's journey to your doorstep.

## P.O. Boxes
Yes, we do deliver to P.O. boxes! All shipments destined for a P.O. box will be routed via USPS.

If you have any further questions about our shipping policies, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.

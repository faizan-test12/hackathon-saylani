# Coffee Brewing Guides

Unlock the best flavors from your Roast & Co. beans with these recommended brewing methods. 

## General Tips for the Best Brew
- **Freshness:** Always use freshly roasted beans, ideally within 2-4 weeks of the roast date.
- **Grind:** Grind your beans just before brewing to preserve the aromatic compounds.
- **Water:** Coffee is 98% water. Use filtered water for a clean taste.
- **Storage:** Store your beans in an airtight container in a cool, dark place. Never freeze or refrigerate your coffee beans, as moisture will ruin the flavor.

## Pour-Over (V60 / Chemex)
A clean, bright cup that highlights the nuanced notes of single-origin coffees.
- **Ratio:** 1:16 (Coffee to Water)
- **Water Temperature:** 200°F
- **Brew Time:** 3-4 minutes
- **Grind:** Medium-fine

## French Press
A full-bodied, rich cup with a heavier mouthfeel.
- **Ratio:** 1:15
- **Water Temperature:** 200°F
- **Brew Time:** 4 minutes steep time
- **Grind:** Coarse

## Espresso
Intense, concentrated coffee with a thick crema.
- **Ratio:** 1:2 (e.g., 18g coffee to 36g liquid espresso)
- **Water Temperature:** 200°F
- **Extraction Time:** 25-30 seconds
- **Grind:** Fine

## AeroPress
Versatile, quick, and smooth. Great for travel.
- **Ratio:** 1:15
- **Method:** Inverted
- **Water Temperature:** 175-185°F
- **Brew Time:** 1.5 minutes
- **Grind:** Medium

## Cold Brew
Smooth, low acidity, and highly caffeinated. Perfect for hot days.
- **Ratio:** 1:8
- **Water Temperature:** Room temperature or cold water
- **Steep Time:** 12-24 hours
- **Grind:** Coarse
- **Serving:** Dilute the resulting concentrate 1:1 with water or milk before drinking.

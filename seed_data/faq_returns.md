# Returns and Refunds Policy

At Roast & Co., we want you to love your coffee as much as we do. That's why we offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on all our products.

## Unopened Bags
If you ordered the wrong item or changed your mind, you can return unopened bags within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Please note that for unopened returns, the customer is responsible for the return shipping costs.

## Opened Bags
We understand that sometimes a coffee just isn't to your taste, or there might be an issue. If you have opened the bag and found it defective or if we sent you the wrong item by mistake, we will gladly offer a full replacement or a full refund at no extra cost to you.

## How to Process a Return
To initiate a return or replacement, please send an email to support@roastandco.com with your order number and a brief explanation of the issue. Our team will guide you through the next steps.

## Refund Timeline
Once we receive your returned item, please allow 5-7 business days for the refund to be processed and appear on your original payment method.

## Non-Returnable Items
Please note that the following items cannot be returned or refunded:
- Items purchased on sale or clearance
- Gift cards

## Items Damaged in Transit
If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery and include clear photos of the damaged package and items. We will arrange a replacement for you immediately.
